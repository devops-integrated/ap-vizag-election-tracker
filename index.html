Reported 16–17 Oct 2026 · Chittoor district

News reports described clashes between TDP and YSRCP supporters, with claims of vehicle attacks and injuries. YSRCP MP P. V. Responsibility and individual allegations should be treated as disputed unless established by investigators or a court.

Official process / court directions reported

APSEC and Supreme Court — review of complaints

21–22 Oct 2026 · Statewide urban local bodies

Reports state APSEC stopped the further election process across all 100 ULBs, withheld final candidate-list publication and sought district-level reports. The Supreme Court directed review of representations and supporting material, including electronic evidence, and consideration of nomination extensions where candidates were prevented from filing because of law-and-order problems.

How each incident is tracked

  • Incident date and location: when and where it was reported.
  • What is claimed: attribute each allegation to the named party, candidate, police or publication.
  • Response / counterclaim: include the other side’s response when reported.
  • Official action: FIR, arrest, police statement, APSEC order or court direction only when sourced.
  • Evidence status: allegation, reported, official action recorded, or established finding—never assume guilt from a complaint.
  • Source trail: direct article or official notice links, with publication date.
