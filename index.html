Represent residents, raise local service problems, take part in meetings and plans, and follow up on approved civic or village works.

How leaders’ statements are assessed

  • Identify who made the statement, when it was made and which event or decision it concerns.

  • Include a relevant response from the other side or the responsible authority when one is available.

A political statement is evidence that a claim was made, not by itself proof that the claim is true.

ROLE GUIDE · URBAN LOCAL BODIES

What a corporator or municipal councillor does

  • Represent the ward and raise residents’ concerns in council and ward-committee meetings.
  • Help identify priorities and scrutinise local plans, resolutions and budgets.
  • Follow up with municipal officials on sanitation, garbage, drainage, water supply, streetlights, roads, parks and public-health services.
  • Ask for information and monitor whether approved works and schemes are being carried out.

A corporator or councillor does not independently run the whole corporation or municipality; implementation and approvals are shared with the relevant officers and governed by law and budgets.

Sources: AP Commissioner & Director of Municipal Administration ↗ · AP Municipal Corporations Act — ward committees ↗

ROLE GUIDE · RURAL LOCAL BODIES

MPTC and ZPTC member responsibilities

MPTC member

Represent a Mandal Parishad Territorial Constituency, raise village and mandal needs, participate in Mandal Parishad deliberations and help review rural development works and schemes within the parishad’s remit.

ZPTC member

Represent a constituency at Zilla Parishad level, raise district rural-development needs, take part in parishad meetings and plans, and follow up on schemes and works entrusted to the Zilla Parishad.

What this can cover

Rural roads, drinking water, sanitation, welfare and development schemes—subject to the relevant body’s legal powers, approved plans and funds.

ROLE GUIDE · GRAM PANCHAYAT

Sarpanch and ward-member responsibilities

Sarpanch

Lead Gram Panchayat work within the law, help convene and lead meetings, represent village priorities, and coordinate local plans and implementation with the Panchayat and responsible officials.

Ward member

Bring ward residents’ concerns to Panchayat meetings, propose or support resolutions, monitor local needs and follow up on decisions.

Typical village priorities

Drinking water, sanitation, drains, village roads, streetlights, public health and eligible development works, depending on the Panchayat’s authority and resources.

A Sarpanch or ward member cannot independently direct every government department or spend funds outside approved powers and procedures.

STATEWIDE ELECTIONS · ROLE GUIDE

What state and national representatives do

MLA · State Assembly

Debate and vote on state laws and budgets, scrutinise the state government, raise constituency issues and take part in committee work.

MP · Lok Sabha

Debate and vote on Union laws and budgets, scrutinise the Union government, work in committees and raise constituency and national matters.

MP · Rajya Sabha

Participate in national legislation and policy scrutiny, represent state and Union Territory interests through the Council of States, and work in parliamentary committees.

MLAs and MPs do not personally administer every local service. Departmental implementation rests with the relevant government and officials. By-election winners serve the remainder of the applicable office term, subject to law.

MPTC
Mandal Parishad Territorial Constituency		Elects a member for a defined area within a mandal.Represents the area in the Mandal Parishad; raises residents’ issues; reviews local development needs; participates in plans and decisions on rural roads, drinking water, sanitation and welfare schemes; monitors whether approved works reach the area. Sarpanch / Ward Member		Village-level local-government election.Sarpanch: leads Panchayat meetings and implementation of lawful village plans and works, coordinates local services and use of approved funds, and represents the village. Municipality / Nagar Panchayat
Councillor / Ward Member		Urban local-government elections for towns and smaller urban areas.Represents ward residents; raises local complaints and proposals; participates in council meetings, budgets and resolutions; follows up with municipal officials on roads, drainage, sanitation, waste, streetlights and town development. MLA
State Legislative Assembly		Voters in an Assembly constituency elect a Member of the Legislative Assembly.Debates and votes on state laws and the state budget; questions and scrutinises the state government; raises constituency concerns; participates in committees where applicable; and advocates for public services and development. MP — Lok Sabha
House of the People		Voters elect a Member of Parliament for a parliamentary constituency.Debates and votes on national laws and the Union budget; questions and scrutinises the Union government; works through parliamentary committees; raises constituency and national issues; and represents constituents’ concerns. MP — Rajya Sabha

By-election
Assembly / Lok Sabha / local body		An election to fill a seat that becomes vacant before the normal term ends.The winner takes on the duties of the specific vacant office—such as corporator, MLA or MP—for the term or period provided by law. Typical nomination checklist

  • Confirm the exact office, constituency/ward and seat reservation category.
  • Check minimum age, citizenship (where required), voter registration and disqualification rules.
  • Use the correct nomination form and submit it to the designated Returning Officer before the deadline.
  • Provide required proposer details, affidavits/declarations, deposit and supporting documents specified in the notification.
  • Check scrutiny results, withdrawal deadlines and the final official list of contesting candidates.
